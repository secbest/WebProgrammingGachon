🚧 The problem: four dead ends

Today, finding and joining Gachon WIND is harder than it should be, especially for international students.

Poor navigation

Unclear instructions make Gachon WIND difficult to find and join, especially for international students.

Missing info

No videos, photos or practice timetables, so it is hard to understand the club's activities and schedules.

Broken links

The club's homepage link on Gachon's CCA page is not working, so club information is hard to reach.

No proper contacts

Without clear contact details, interested students are discouraged from joining the club.

What exists today? Two sources, neither complete

🏫

Gachon CCA page

The official club listing, but the homepage link is broken.

📸

Instagram (@GCU_WIND (opens in a new tab))

Real team photos, but updates are limited.

Key gap: no practice schedule, no sign-up form, and no clear contact details.

💡 Our solution

Three ideas that turn scattered, outdated info into one site that always works.

Why it's better

Always current

The CCA page is static and Instagram rarely posts; our schedule and gallery stay up to date.

Easy to join

Neither existing option has a sign-up form or working contact; ours takes a few clicks.

Truly accessible

Four languages and a mobile-first layout, so every Gachon student can use it.

🗺️ Four-phase plan

From collecting content to handing the site over to the club.

  1. PLAN & DESIGNCollect club content, site map and wireframes.
  2. DEVELOPHTML & CSS pages, then JavaScript for the schedule, gallery, form and languages.
  3. TEST & FEEDBACKPhones, browsers, WIND members and students.
  4. LAUNCHFix issues, publish, and hand it to the club to keep updated.
