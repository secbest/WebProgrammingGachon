Korean Bibimbap Recipe

What You Need

  • 2 cups cooked warm white rice
  • Blanched spinach and bean sprouts
  • 1 carrot, thinly julienned and sautéed
  • 1 fresh egg
  • Toasted sesame oil and seeds

Steps

  1. Cook the rice and scoop a serving into a wide serving bowl.
  2. Sauté and season each vegetable separately with a pinch of salt.
  3. Warning: Keep the pan on medium-low heat when frying the sunny-side-up egg to avoid burning the edges.
  4. Carefully arrange each prepared vegetable mound side-by-side around the bowl.
  5. Add a spoonful of gochujang (chili paste) and a splash of sesame oil, then mix everything thoroughly before eating.
